# Progression phases research

Research date: 2026-10-21. Scope: Repentance and Repentance+, one save slot. This document proposes a product model; it does not implement one or change any save. Earlier editions need their own profiles.

## Recommendation

Use ten phases as an ordered roadmap, backed by independent milestones. Isaac has real content gates, but its routes form a branching graph. The phase order below is an editorial recommendation, not a claim that the game requires every earlier phase before every later one.

Keep three separate answers:

1. **Verified progression frontier:** the furthest milestone directly supported by this slot's gameplay data.
2. **Recommended next objective:** a useful unfinished objective, with its actual prerequisites.
3. **Content availability:** a separate reference view that may use save secret flags. It must not advance the verified progression frontier.

Do not use hours played, deaths, item count, or tier-list ranks as proxies for a route phase or player skill.

## The ten-phase roadmap

The completion column describes the exit milestone for a phase. These predicates are independent: a late victory must not manufacture proof of earlier counters or historical actions.

| Phase | Player-facing goal | Completion evidence | Reason for this position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. First ending | Defeat Mom and begin progressing beyond Depths. | Explicit confirmation of the first Mom milestone. The current catalog leaves this action manual; see the early-save ambiguity below. | Initial ordinary chapter gate. |
| 2. Heart cycle | Reach 11 credited Heart / It Lives! victories. | Heart counter at least 11. | Establishes reliable access to the two classic branches; Hush becomes a side objective at 10. |
| 3. Photographs | Obtain both regular Chapter 6 routes: Isaac 5 times and Satan 5 times. | Isaac counter at least 5 AND Satan counter at least 5. | Recommended foundation for repeatable classic-route runs. One photograph already provides a conventional Home-door solution later; both are not required for Mother or Beast. |
| 4. Hush and the secret exit | Defeat Hush three times. | Hush counter at least 3. | First victory opens Void; third opens the alternate path. Can be pursued before phase 3 is finished. |
| 5. Mother | Complete the alternate route and defeat Mother once. | Mother credited-victory counter above zero OR a Mother completion mark. | Opens the ordinary Home route. |
| 6. Home and Beast | Learn the Ascent, use Home to start opening Tainted characters, and defeat Beast once. | Beast credited-victory counter above zero OR a Beast completion mark. Tainted unlocks are separate milestones, not part of this predicate. | Completes the ordinary Repentance story-route expansion. |
| 7. Route coverage | Complete every one of the 12 objective types at least once across the roster, using Greedier for the Greed objective. | For each objective, at least one corresponding mark; an appropriate credited-victory counter can prove a non-Greedier objective independently. | Brings unfinished classic bosses, Mega Satan, Delirium and Boss Rush into one finite cleanup phase. They can all have been completed earlier. |
| 8. Character completion | Finish every Hard / Greedier objective on all 34 characters. | All 408 character/objective cells meet their required difficulty. | Exact Death Certificate milestone, without an arbitrary percentage cutoff. |
| 9. Remaining conditions | Finish all non-collection unlock conditions, including all 45 numbered challenges and special tasks. | All relevant conditions independently satisfied; completed challenge flags prove challenge completion. Manual-only history stays unknown without confirmation. | Separates character marks from the other requirements for full completion. Challenges and special tasks should already have been pursued in parallel. |
| 10. Collection and Dead God | Pick up every remaining required item and verify full completion. | Phase 9 conditions AND the edition's required collection flags. | Final cleanup; already collected items count throughout the entire roadmap. Once satisfied, display a completed Dead God state. |

Phases 3 and 7 are recommended coverage goals, not mandatory prerequisites for the Hush/Mother/Home branch. Phases 8-10 are a completionist order; their tasks can overlap. A player can finish the collection before the last character mark, for example.

The 12 objective types are Heart / It Lives!, Isaac, Satan, Boss Rush, ???, The Lamb, Mega Satan, Hush, Greed / Greedier, Delirium, Mother, and Beast. Phase 7 measures global coverage, not twelve marks on one character. Phase 8 measures every character separately. Hard completes the corresponding ordinary objective; Greedier is the harder state of the single Greed cell, not a thirteenth objective. Sources: [Completion Marks](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Completion_Marks), [Death Certificate](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Death_Certificate), [Greedier](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Greedier_Mode).

### Heart submilestones

Display the heart cycle as concrete progress rather than eleven indistinguishable runs:

- First credited victory: the Heart route has been completed at least once.
- Fifth: Everything Is Terrible increases baseline game difficulty.
- Tenth: Blue Womb / Hush access becomes available under ordinary rules.
- Eleventh: It Lives! and regular Sheol/Cathedral exits.

The fifth victory is a useful submilestone, not a new route phase. Sources: [Endings: Heart ending 5](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Endings), [Blue Womb](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Blue_Womb), [It Lives!](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/It_Lives%21).

### Actual branching dependencies

The ordinary route structure is:

```text
Mom -> Womb / Heart cycle
  |
  +-- Heart 11 -> regular Isaac / Satan routes
  |                  +-- Isaac 5 -> Polaroid -> Chest / ???
  |                  +-- Satan 5 -> Negative -> Dark Room / Lamb
  |                                           |
  |                  either Chapter 6 final boss -> Mega Satan route
  |
  +-- Heart 10 -> Hush
                     +-- Hush 1 -> Void / Delirium
                     +-- Hush 3 -> alternate path -> Mother 1
                                                      |
                                                      +-> Strange Door / Ascent / Home
                                                            +-> Tainted unlocks
                                                            +-> Dogma -> Beast

Greed -> donations -> Greedier / Lost upgrade / Keeper
Characters, numbered challenges and special tasks progress in parallel.
```

The photograph is consumed by the Strange Door. The ordinary route therefore requires teleporting out after Mom; the marked skull supplies The Fool. Alternative door-opening methods exist. Mother access normally uses both Knife Pieces and the flesh door after Mausoleum Mom. Individual run actions are instructions for the next attempt, not facts reconstructible from a single persistent save. Sources: [A Strange Door](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/A_Strange_Door), [Corpse](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Corpse).

### Evidence rules

1. Use only `progress`: credited counters, decoded completion marks, completed challenge flags, collection and carefully interpreted bestiary observations.
2. Exact repeat thresholds require their credited counters. A mark proves a completed objective, not how many times it was repeated. Do not sum different characters' marks into a substitute total kill count.
3. For a single boss victory, the existing model accepts a positive credited counter OR the corresponding character mark. Preserve both sources and report conflicts rather than silently repairing the data.
4. A bestiary encounter or player death proves neither victory nor a route unlock. Entity kills are not interchangeable with credited progression wins: multi-part bosses and appearances in other contexts need separate interpretation. Do not use bestiary totals for thresholds 5, 10, 11 or 3.
5. An item pickup proves that pickup. It does not independently prove that the item's normal unlock condition was performed in this slot. In particular, a collected Polaroid is not five proven Isaac victories.
6. A Tainted character's own mark proves recorded progress as that character. It does not reconstruct a closet interaction; lack of marks does not establish that the character is locked or never played.
7. Conditions have three states: satisfied, missing, unknown. A supported counter below a repeat threshold means the threshold is missing in the recorded progress; an unavailable metric or unrecorded historical action is unknown.
8. Manual confirmations are explicitly labeled user evidence, separate from automatic save evidence. Never write them into the source save.
9. Secret flags and Steam achievements never contribute to the phase or its accomplished milestones. Keep the existing Content availability mode visibly separate. The origin of a secret flag cannot be established from one save.

### Early-save ambiguity

There is no verified dedicated credited Mom counter in the current model. The misleadingly named counter 1 counts Heart / It Lives!. The current Womb node uses manual confirmation, with a Heart victory as later-route evidence. A fresh-looking snapshot can therefore mean either no Mom victory or a Mom victory with no subsequently recorded Heart victory.

Do not automatically label either case as a proven failure to beat Mom. Display "Early progression; first Mom milestone not automatically established" until stronger evidence or an explicit confirmation is available. Later-route evidence can advance the progression frontier without claiming a recovered history of how the player reached that route. Bestiary sightings do not remove this ambiguity; adopting bestiary kills as credited Mom evidence would require additional format/context validation.

### Frontier and next objective

Evaluate milestones independently first. Track both entry evidence and exit milestones: a Hush victory is evidence of being in phase 4 even before the third victory. For phases 2-8, the greatest directly supported entry or exit milestone establishes how far the roadmap has been demonstrated. A completed later phase can supersede earlier phases for navigation without marking their conditions satisfied. Phases 9-10 are aggregate completion milestones and require their full conjunctions.

Remain in the furthest phase with direct entry evidence until its exit objective is complete; otherwise display the next phase after the furthest completed exit. Skip already satisfied objectives. For this purpose, a credited Heart victory anchors phase 2; an Isaac or Satan victory anchors phase 3; a Hush victory anchors phase 4; three Hush victories lead to phase 5; a Mother victory leads to phase 6; and a Beast victory leads to phase 7. Tainted-character marks independently demonstrate activity in the Home/Tainted part of phase 6, without proving a Beast or Mother victory. Merely having Blue Womb access at Heart 10 is a concurrent opportunity, not evidence that Hush has been attempted or completed. An encounter may support an optional "attempted" label but cannot satisfy a completion milestone.

Retain unfinished earlier milestones as parallel work. If an earlier milestone is unknown, keep it unknown; it must not pull a directly demonstrated late-game player back into a beginner label. Record the exact anchor and its provenance with the label so this rule stays explainable.

Examples:

| Recorded facts | Main focus | Parallel or uncertain work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heart 11, Isaac 0, Satan 1, Hush 0 | Phase 3: photographs | Hush is a valid concurrent target; Boss Rush does not change the route phase. |
| Heart 10, one credited Hush victory, neither photograph threshold reached | Phase 4: Hush, one of three | Heart 11 and photographs remain independent unfinished milestones. |
| Hush 3, neither photograph threshold reached, Mother 0 | Phase 5: Mother | Finish photographs separately; they are not a Mother prerequisite. |
| Mother victory, Beast 0 | Phase 6: Home / Beast | Tainted unlocks can be pursued during Home attempts. |
| Beast mark, Hush counter below 3 | Phase 7: remaining route coverage | Preserve the Hush threshold as unfinished/conflicting history; do not invent three wins. |
| One Tainted character has a mark, Beast 0 | Home/Tainted progress is independently demonstrated | Beast remains unfinished; no closet interaction or Mother victory is fabricated. |
| All 408 Hard / Greedier cells complete, some challenges incomplete | Phase 9: remaining conditions | Collection cleanup can happen concurrently. |
| All conditions verified, required collection incomplete | Phase 10: collection | Show the exact missing item IDs. |
| All secrets set, gameplay progress empty | Early progression with no gameplay milestones verified | Availability may be extensive in its separate reference view. |

The first example matches the existing slot 1 report, not a new read of the player's current game. That report records Heart 11, Isaac 0, Satan 1, Hush 0 and one Boss Rush completion. Its Hush encounter and death do not establish a Hush victory. Source: [saved slot 1 report](../reports/rep_persistentgamedata1.json).

## Parallel tracks that should stay visible

**Greed and donations.** Useful thresholds are 500 for Greedier, 879 for Lost's starting Holy Mantle, and 1000 for Keeper. These are priorities alongside the story roadmap. A positive present counter can support a reached threshold, but a below-threshold reading must not erase a historical unlock if that counter can reset or roll over. The 1000th donation explodes the machine; confirm exact persistent-counter behavior before using a low value as proof that Keeper's condition never happened. A Greedier mark directly proves that mode's completion, without reconstructing 500 donations. Source: [Greed Donation Machine](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Greed_Donation_Machine).

**Characters and starting upgrades.** Track proven character-specific conditions and progress independently. Examples include opening Tainted characters in Home and earning Lost's starting protection. Do not make a generic "all characters unlocked" phase depend on secret flags. Some unlock histories need manual confirmation.

**Numbered challenges and useful rewards.** Work on these throughout the roadmap, using the completed flags, not challenge availability. There are 45 in the repository's Repentance profile. Daily challenges are a separate category; completing the 45 numbered challenges does not cover all daily-related conditions.

**Timed routes.** Boss Rush normally requires Mom before 20:00 on the main route, or Mausoleum/Gehenna Mom before 25:00. Hush normally requires Heart / It Lives! before 30:00 after Blue Womb is unlocked. These are per-run access instructions, not extra persistent phase counters. Sources: [Boss Rush](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Boss_Rush), [Blue Womb](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Blue_Womb).

**High-value items.** Existing S-F ranks can prioritize recommendations within a phase. Rank must not determine whether the phase is complete.

## Edition and completion limits

Repentance and Repentance+ share the central route gates above. Repentance+ changes some Void access behavior; avoid hardcoding Repentance portal probabilities into general phase descriptions. Mother portal behavior in particular has version and prior-victory qualifications. The stable milestone is a credited boss victory, not a presumed portal appearance. Sources: [The Void](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Void), [Game Progression](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Progression).

Repentance+ completion marks contain separate offline and online difficulty bits. Use the existing decoder; raw value 4 is online Normal, not Hard. See [format profiles](save-formats.md).

For Repentance, the current project models 636 non-Dead-God conditions and 717 visible required collection entries. For Repentance+, its adapter adds four conditions, 638-641, producing 640 non-Dead-God conditions. This is a count of requirements in the edition profile, not a count of secret flags used as evidence. Some compound or historical requirements remain manual. See [Dead God schema](unlock-schema.md#the-dead-god-goal) and [the game's achievement requirement list](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Achievements).

Consequently, an automatic analysis can establish "all marks complete" or "collection complete" while full Dead God verification remains unknown. It must not convert unknown historical conditions into success because a Dead God secret flag is set. Likewise, three completed slots / Infinity% are multi-slot goals outside this single-slot phase sequence. Source: [Save File Completion](https://bindingofisaacrebirth.wiki.gg/wiki/Save_File_Completion).

## Acceptance examples for a future implementation

These are research requirements, not tests implemented by this change:

- Heart 9, 10 and 11 produce distinct route milestones; 10 must not be treated as the permanent Sheol/Cathedral gate.
- Isaac 4/5, Satan 4/5 and Hush 0/1/2/3 cross only their own thresholds.
- Hush three times on one character satisfies the alternate-path threshold.
- A Mother mark proves Mother even if its general counter is inconsistent; no missing Hush wins are generated.
- An unlocked Tainted character does not prove a Beast victory.
- A Greed mark does not satisfy Greedier coverage or the Hard/Greedier matrix.
- A Repentance+ raw mark value of 4 does not satisfy a Hard requirement.
- A Hush encounter/death with zero credited wins does not advance the Hush milestone.
- A collected photograph does not prove its five-victory unlock condition.
- All 408 marks alone do not verify Dead God; challenge flags, other conditions and collection still matter.
- Mutating only secret flags, including setting every secret on an empty slot, leaves all phase accomplishments and the verified frontier unchanged.
- A late boss victory with unfinished early thresholds shows advanced progression plus explicit gaps, rather than fabricated early history.

No implementation, build, test-suite change, or source-save modification was performed for this research.
